The family library is a few sites that work together. You only need to remember this one: {{ cfg.HOME_URL }}. Everything else is linked from here.

The sites

  • Library portal ({{ cfg.PORTAL_URL }}): your home page. What you are reading, what arrived, search, getting books, comics, following series, your devices and settings.
  • Library site ({{ cfg.BOOKS_URL }}): Calibre-Web, where the books live. Read in the browser, make shelves, and it is what your Kobo syncs with.
  • Audiobookshelf ({{ cfg.AUDIO_URL }}): audiobooks, in the browser or its app, and it remembers where you are.
  • Shelfmark ({{ cfg.SHELF_URL }}): for when you want to choose the exact copy of a book yourself.

Signing in

{% if cfg.AUTHELIA_ENABLED %}

You sign in once, on the sign-in page, with your library user name and password{% if admin %} and, because you are an admin, a second factor (an authenticator app, a passkey or a security key){% endif %}. After that every site above lets you straight in for the rest of the session. Your session ends after a while of not using it; signing in again is one page.

{% else %}

Each site asks for the same library user name and password. (When the admin turns on the sign-in page, one sign-in will cover them all.)

{% endif %}

Apps and devices sign in on their own: your Kobo uses its sync link, reading apps use your library login, the Audiobookshelf app its own sign-in. None of them need the sign-in page.

Your books are yours

Everyone has their own library. You see only books you asked for, uploaded, or added. When you ask for a book the library already holds, it is simply added to yours: nothing is downloaded twice (unless the reader who has it keeps their books private).

First steps

  1. On the start page, tick the devices you read on (which Kobo, which Kindle, a phone or tablet): comics are then made for their screens, and the start page shows the steps and apps for each.
  2. Open Devices and set up what you read on: link the Kobo, save the Kindle address.
  3. Turn on notifications there, so you hear when something you asked for has arrived.
  4. Search for a book on the portal and press Get it (see Getting a book).
