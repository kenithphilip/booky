You can hear about what you asked for without checking the portal: when a book or comic has arrived, when a copy is waiting for your yes, when something is held for you to check, and when something you follow came out.

On your phone (recommended)

  1. Install the free ntfy app (Android or iPhone).
  2. On Devices → Phone notifications → Turn on. You get your own private topic.
  3. In ntfy, tap + and subscribe to that topic (Devices shows it, and the server when it is not ntfy.sh). Or open the subscribe link on your phone.

Anyone who knows your topic name could read your notifications (they only name books), so keep it to yourself; Devices can give you a new one.

By mail

Devices → E-mail me when a request is ready or denied; followed series send one short digest.
