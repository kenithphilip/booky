Phones and tablets read the library's files as they are: nothing is converted for them. Tick yours on the start page and it shows the apps for it. Most apps connect to the library through its OPDS catalog: {{ cfg.BOOKS_URL }}/opds/ (keep the slash at the end), with your library user name and password.

iPhone and iPad

  • Books: {{ device_apps.ios.books }}.
  • Comics: {{ device_apps.ios.comics }}. On an iPad, turning it sideways shows two pages, or a wide page whole.
  • Audiobooks: {{ device_apps.ios.audio }}: server {{ cfg.AUDIO_URL }}, your library login.

Android phone and tablet

  • Books: {{ device_apps.android.books }}.
  • Comics: {{ device_apps.android.comics }}.
  • Audiobooks: {{ device_apps.android.audio }}: server {{ cfg.AUDIO_URL }}, your library login.

Any device

  • In the browser: open the library site and press Read on a book.
  • Downloading: My books → Download, in the format you choose on Devices (EPUB, KEPUB, PDF...). A comic's page offers its CBZ.
  • KOReader progress: KOReader's built-in progress sync does not work with this library; Devices explains the plugin that does.

Mark what you read

Apps on a phone or tablet do not report your progress to the library: mark books Read on their page in the portal.
