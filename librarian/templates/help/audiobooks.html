Listening

  • Android: the Audiobookshelf app, server {{ cfg.AUDIO_URL }}, your library user name and password.
  • iPhone / iPad: there is no official app yet (only a test version). ShelfPlayer, SoundLeaf and AudioBooth work well: same address and login.
  • Browser: Audiobookshelf plays in any browser and keeps your place between devices.
  • Any other player: My audiobooks → Download: one file, or a ZIP of the folder.

Getting audiobooks

Search on the portal; on a book's page choose Get the audiobook (series and author pages under Following have an Audiobook button too). The portal finds a copy (M4B first, unabridged, your language) and shows it to you to confirm. What arrives is checked before it goes into your audiobooks: its title, and, when Hardcover knows the book's audiobook length, its length, so an abridged or partial copy is held for you to decide. (A download that arrives as a ZIP is checked once unpacked by name only.) Shelfmark lets you pick one yourself.

A very large audiobook can stall on the way; if Requests says the download failed, the portal tries another copy, and the admin can also press Retry for it in Shelfmark.

Hardcover

With your Hardcover token set on Devices, what you listen to shows on Hardcover as Currently Reading, then Read, with the minutes listened, within about 10 minutes.

A lost phone

On Audiobookshelf open your account settings and use Log out all devices, then change your password on Devices.
