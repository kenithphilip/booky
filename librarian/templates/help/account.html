A lost phone or tablet

  1. Change your password (Devices → Account).
  2. Audiobookshelf: account settings → Log out all devices.
  3. A lost Kobo: Devices → Kobo → Regenerate link (the old one stops working).

Privacy

Your library is yours: no other reader sees which books you have or read. The library's admin, who runs it, can see every library (to fix problems); nobody else can. {% if cfg.FAMILY_SHARING %}When you look up a book another reader has, the portal can say "ready now, no download" (never who has it); getting it adds that one copy to your library too.

Keeping your books private

On the start page (or Devices), Keep my books private means no book you have is ever offered to anyone else: not as "ready now", not when they request it (they download their own copy). Your books stay yours either way; nobody else can see, open or download them.{% else %}Each reader's copies are their own.{% endif %}
