The library knows what you are reading from the Kobo (and KOReader, and the library site's own reader). Everything else you can mark yourself.

  • Mark a book: its page → Mark: Read · Reading · Unread. It shows on the portal, on your Kobo after its next sync, and reaches your trackers.
  • A comic series: read up to here next to a volume marks it and every earlier one you have.

Trackers (all optional, on Devices)

  • Hardcover (books and audiobooks): paste a token from hardcover.app → Settings → API. Kobo and KOReader progress go there, and so does your audiobook listening.
  • AniList (manga): Connect AniList; manga volumes you finish count as read there. The number only goes up.
  • Metron (Western comics): your Metron user name and API key; issues you finish are marked read in your Metron collection.
