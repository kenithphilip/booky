A Kindle cannot sync with the library like a Kobo, so books go to it by Amazon's Send to Kindle e-mail, or through its web browser.

Set up Send to Kindle (once)

  1. Find your Kindle's address: on amazon.com (or your country's Amazon) → Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings. It ends in @kindle.com.
  2. On the same page, add the library's sending address to the Approved Personal Document E-mail List. Devices shows which address to approve.
  3. Enter your Kindle address on Devices and press Send a test. It arrives within a few minutes if the approval worked.

Sending books

  • Any book's page → Send to Kindle. The portal fixes the EPUB first so Amazon accepts it.
  • Devices → Preferences → Automatically e-mail every new ebook to my Kindle: everything that arrives for you goes to the Kindle by itself.
  • Comics are converted for the Kindle Colorsoft when they are sent (split into parts if very large).
  • Amazon's limit is about 50 MB per mail, and the portal sends at most 20 a day for you.

Through the Kindle's browser (no mail, no size limit)

On the Kindle open the Experimental Browser at {{ cfg.PORTAL_URL }}/send, then on your phone use Send to an e-reader on the book's page with the code it shows. A Kindle's browser only opens AZW3, MOBI, PDF and TXT, never EPUB: on the book's page, pick AZW3 and press Convert first when needed.

Reading progress

A Kindle does not report what you read. Mark books yourself on their page (Mark: Read · Reading · Unread), or read up to here on a comic series; your trackers (Hardcover, AniList, Metron) follow.
