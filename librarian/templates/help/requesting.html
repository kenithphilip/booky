Search on the portal for a title or an author and open the book. Its page offers, depending on what exists:

  • Request on a free copy (Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Internet Archive, LibriVox): it arrives within minutes.
  • Get it: the portal looks for a copy on the library's download sources. It picks the right title and author, one book (no box sets or abridged copies), EPUB first, in your language, and shows it to you before anything downloads: the release name, its size, where it is from and why it was chosen. Yes, that one downloads it; Not it never offers that release again and looks for another. Audiobooks: Get the audiobook, the same way.
  • Pick in Shelfmark: Shelfmark opens already searching for the book and you choose the copy yourself.
  • Keep looking: for a book no free catalogue has yet; it checks again on a schedule and requests it when it appears.

After you say yes

  1. The download runs (usually minutes; large ones longer). Requests shows how far it got.
  2. When the file arrives it is checked: the title, author, language and ISBN inside it must match the book you asked for. If it does not, it is held for you instead of going into your library: Keep it anyway or Not it: find another.
  3. It lands in your library, then on your Kobo at its next sync, or your Kindle if you send it (or automatically).

When something is not right

  • Wrong book arrived? Its page → Not the book you asked for? → Wrong book. It leaves your library, that copy is never offered again, and the portal looks for another (asking you first).
  • Not found after a few days: the sources name it differently. Try Pick in Shelfmark.
  • Waiting for approval: the admin approves requests on this library; you are told when it goes ahead.

Ready now, no download

A book (or comic) the library already holds shows as ready now, no download: Add to mine puts it in yours at once. It never says whose it is, and a reader who keeps their books private (their device settings) is never counted.
