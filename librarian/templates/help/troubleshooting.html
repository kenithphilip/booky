Find what you see below. Each answer says why it happens and what to do. Words you do not know are explained in What the words mean. Still stuck? Tell your library admin what you did and what you saw (a screenshot helps).

Signing in

  • I forgot my password. Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in page (or open {{ cfg.PORTAL_URL }}/forgot), type your user name or e-mail address, and open the link that arrives (it works once, for 30 minutes). The new password works everywhere at once: the library, the sign-in page, the audiobook app and your reading apps. No e-mail? Check spam, ask again, or ask your library admin.
  • It says my password is wrong, and now it will not let me try. After {{ cfg.LOCKOUT_FAILS }} wrong tries the sign-in pauses for {{ (cfg.LOCKOUT_SECONDS // 60) }} minutes, to keep guessers out. Wait, then type it carefully (the user name is lower case). Still refused: ask your admin.
    • {% if cfg.AUTHELIA_ENABLED %}
  • It asks for a code or a passkey and I do not have my phone. Use another second step you set up (a passkey on another device, a security key). Without one, ask your library admin for help. Once back in, add a spare at sign-in settings so it cannot happen again.
  • The sign-in page keeps coming back. Your browser is not keeping the sign-in: allow cookies for this site, turn off "block all cookies" or private browsing, and sign in again. On an iPhone: Settings → Safari → turn off "Block All Cookies".
    • {% endif %}
  • The page says the library is restarting, or does not load. Wait a minute and reload. If it stays like that, tell your admin.

Kobo

  • A book is in My books but not on the Kobo. Open the book's page: it says where it stands on your Kobo, with a button to Send to my Kobo. Then sync the Kobo (Wi-Fi on, tap the Sync icon at the top of the Home screen).
  • A book disappeared from the Kobo. It is still in your library: you finished it and your finished-books setting took it off, or you removed it on the Kobo, or your Kobo gets only the books you send. Its page → Send to my Kobo brings it back to stay. My books → Not on my Kobo lists them all.
  • The Kobo says it cannot sync, or nothing arrives at all. On Devices → Kobo press Test my link:
    • "Your Kobo link works": the library is fine; check the Kobo's Wi-Fi, then check its config file still holds your link (below).
    • "The library refused this link": the link was regenerated. Copy the new one into the Kobo's config file.
    • "You have no Kobo link yet": press Generate my Kobo link and follow the Kobo guide.
    • "did not answer" or "answered HTTP…": the library is busy or has a problem: try again in a minute, then tell your admin.
  • It worked, then stopped after I signed out, signed in or reset the Kobo. Those rewrite the Kobo's config file and remove your link. Put it back: Kobo guide, Link it.
  • My place in the book is not saved. The Kobo sends your place when it syncs: sync before you switch devices. A book downloaded as a plain EPUB saves your place only per chapter; KEPUB (the Kobo's own format) saves the exact page.
  • A comic looks wrong on the Kobo (pages cut, too small, sideways). Its page → Pages on e-readers → choose another layout. The Kobo keeps the copy it has until you download it again: press and hold its cover → Remove download, then tap it.
  • The Kobo store or OverDrive stopped working. That is expected while the Kobo is linked to the library. Unlinking gives them back.

Kindle

  • Nothing arrives, not even the test. Almost always the approved list: on Amazon (Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings) the Approved Personal Document E-mail List must hold {% if kindle_sender() %}{{ kindle_sender() }}{% else %}the library's sending address (Devices shows it){% endif %} exactly, and the Kindle address saved on Devices must be the one Amazon shows for that Kindle (it ends in @kindle.com). Fix either, then Send a test again and give it five minutes.
  • Amazon e-mailed me asking to verify a document. Amazon sometimes asks the account holder to confirm a new document: press its Verify link, and the book arrives.
  • The test arrived, but a book did not. The book may be larger than Amazon takes by mail ({{ cfg.KINDLE_MAX_MB }} MB): comics are sent in parts, a very large book may not go at all. The portal says so on the book's page; your Kindle's web browser can fetch it instead (Kindle guide).
  • A finished book is still on the Kindle. Amazon lets nobody else take books off a Kindle: delete it on the Kindle (press and hold → Remove from Device). My books reminds you of the ones you finished.

Phone, tablet and downloads

  • I downloaded a book and my phone cannot open it. Pick the format your app reads: EPUB for Apple Books and almost every reading app, CBZ for comics (Panels, Chunky, CDisplayEx), PDF only for books that are PDFs. Set your usual format on Devices → Preferences.
  • A reading app will not connect to the library. Use the catalog address exactly: {{ cfg.BOOKS_URL }}/opds/ (with the / at the end), and your library user name and password.
    • {% if cfg.AUDIO_URL %}
  • The audiobook app will not sign in. The server is {{ cfg.AUDIO_URL }}, with your library user name and password. On an iPhone use ShelfPlayer, SoundLeaf or AudioBooth (there is no official iPhone app yet).
    • {% endif %}

Getting books

  • My request says "looking" for days. No good copy was found yet; it keeps looking (within the hour, then every few hours, then daily). Very new books often appear a few days after release. Try Pick in Shelfmark to choose a copy yourself.
  • It says "confirm this copy". The library found a copy and shows you what it is before it downloads: press Yes if it is the book you want.
  • It says "check this file". What arrived did not look like the book you asked for; it is held, not added. Keep it anyway, or say it is not it and the library looks again.
  • The wrong book, edition or language arrived. Its page → Wrong book: it leaves your library and the library finds another copy, asking you first. Set the language you read in on Devices → Preferences.
  • Search finds nothing. Try the author's surname and one word of the title.

Notifications

  • My phone gets no notifications. In the ntfy app, check you subscribed to your own topic (Devices shows it) and that the app may send notifications (your phone's Settings). Devices → Send a test shows whether the library can reach you.
  • I get no e-mails. Check the address on Devices → Account, the "E-mail me" box under Preferences, and your spam folder.
