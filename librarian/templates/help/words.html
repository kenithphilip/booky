The words the library uses, in plain language.

  • Shelfmark ({{ cfg.SHELF_URL }}): a search where you choose the exact copy yourself. For when the portal finds nothing.

Getting books

  • Get it: the portal finds a good copy for you (the right book, your language, a format your devices read).
  • Confirm this copy: the portal shows you the copy it found before anything downloads; you say yes or "not this one".
  • Ready now, no download: the library already holds it; Add to mine puts it in your library at once.
  • Pick in Shelfmark: choose a copy yourself, in Shelfmark.
  • Keep looking: the library searches again by itself (within the hour, then every few hours, then daily) until a copy turns up.
  • Check this file: what arrived did not look like what you asked for, so it waits for you instead of being added.
  • Following / New for you: follow a series or an author, and what comes out next shows on your portal home page.

File types

  • EPUB: the usual ebook file. Almost every reading app, Kobo and Kindle (by e-mail) read it.
  • KEPUB: the Kobo's own kind of EPUB; it remembers your exact page.
  • AZW3 / MOBI: older Kindle files, only copied to a Kindle by USB cable.
  • PDF: fixed pages, like paper; fine on a tablet, hard to read on a small screen.
  • CBZ / CBR: a comic: its pages as pictures. Comic apps read them; the library makes a Kobo copy of each.
  • Fixed layout: a book whose pages stay as drawn (comics), instead of text that re-flows to your screen.

Devices

  • Sync: the Kobo asks the library what is new and fetches it (tap the Sync icon, or it happens by itself on Wi-Fi).
  • Kobo sync link: your private address that connects your Kobo to your library. Keep it to yourself; Devices can make a new one.
  • Kobo copy: a version of a comic made for your Kobo's screen.
  • Send to Kindle: Amazon's service that delivers a book e-mailed to your Kindle's own address (it ends in @kindle.com).
  • Approved Personal Document E-mail List: the list on Amazon of addresses allowed to send books to your Kindle. The library's {% if kindle_sender() %}{{ kindle_sender() }}{% else %}sending address{% endif %} must be on it.
  • OPDS catalog: an address a reading app uses to browse and download your books ({{ cfg.BOOKS_URL }}/opds/).
  • ntfy: a free phone app that shows the library's notifications.
