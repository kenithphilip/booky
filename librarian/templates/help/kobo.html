A Kobo gets your books by itself once it is linked: every book in your library, and every new one, arrives on its next sync. Comics come as proper fixed-layout books, made for your Kobo's screen (in colour on a Libra Colour or Clara Colour).

Link it (once)

  1. On the portal open Devices and copy your Kobo sync link. Press Test my link to check it.
  2. Connect the Kobo to a computer by USB. Open the .kobo/Kobo/Kobo eReader.conf file on it with a text editor.
  3. Find the line starting api_endpoint= (in the [OneStoreServices] section) and replace everything after the = with your sync link. Save, eject, and sync the Kobo.

While linked, the Kobo store and OverDrive do not work on that Kobo. A brand-new Kobo pairs fine since the library moved to Calibre-Web v4.0.8.

Day to day

  • New books arrive on the next sync (tap the sync icon, or it syncs by itself on Wi-Fi).
  • Your place is saved to the library, so the portal can show "Reading 45 %", and, with a Hardcover token, your Hardcover account too.
  • Finished on the Kobo shows as Read on the portal; Read marked on the portal shows as Finished on the Kobo after its next sync.
  • Only some books on the Kobo? Your settings → Only the books I send (below).
  • Deleted a book on the Kobo but want it back? It stays in your library: its page on the portal says so and offers Put it back on my Kobo; it returns at the next sync.
  • Removing a book from your library (its page → Remove) deletes it from the Kobo at its next sync (Wi-Fi on, Sync). If it is still there after that: My Books → press and hold the cover → Remove → Remove from My Books.

Keeping your Kobo tidy

With hundreds of books in your library, you may want the Kobo to hold only what you are reading and what is next. Everything is set on the start page → Your settings (or Devices), and each reader chooses for themself:

  • What goes to your Kobo: Every book in my library (new books arrive by themselves) or Only the books I send (nothing arrives by itself; a book's page → Send to my Kobo). Switching keeps what is on the Kobo now, unless you untick that. On the Kobo, books you send show in a collection called From the library.
  • Finished books: never, right away, or 7 or 30 days after you finish one (the Kobo says so, or you marked it Read), it leaves the Kobo at a sync. It stays in your library with your place and your Read mark.
  • Getting one back: its page → Send to my Kobo (or Put it back on my Kobo for one you deleted on the Kobo). A book you send back stays: it is not taken off by itself again. My books → Not on my Kobo lists them all.
  • Taking one off by hand: its page → Take it off my Kobo (or delete it on the Kobo: My Books → press and hold → Remove). Either way it stays in your library, and the page offers to send it back.
  • A sync never re-sends books already on the Kobo: only new ones, and changes to your reading marks.

Comics on your Kobo

  • Tell the library which Kobo you have on the start page: comics are made for its screen (a Sage or an Elipsa gets sharper pages than a Clara), in colour on a Libra Colour or Clara Colour, in greyscale on a black-and-white Kobo (smaller, and made for e-ink).
  • How the pages are laid out is chosen from the comic's own pages: two-page spreads come as each half in turn, then the whole spread turned sideways; a landscape book (every page wide, like The Complete Peanuts) has its pages turned, never cut; webtoons are cut into screens.
  • Pages turned sideways: lock the Kobo's screen rotation to portrait, then turn the Kobo to read them.
  • Looks wrong? On the comic's page, Pages on e-readers lets you choose another layout; the Kobo copy is made again. Your Kobo keeps the copy it has until you download it again: press and hold the cover → Remove download (not Remove from My Books), then tap the book.

Without linking: send one book

On the Kobo open the web browser (Settings → Beta features → Web browser) at {{ cfg.PORTAL_URL }}/send. It shows a 4-letter code. On your phone, open the book in My books → Send to an e-reader → type the code. The Kobo's page offers the download a few seconds later.
