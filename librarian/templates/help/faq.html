I asked for a book and it has not arrived

Open Requests. Confirm this copy means it waits for your yes; looking means no copy was found yet (it looks again within the hour, then every few hours, then daily); downloading is under way; check this file means what arrived did not look like the book.

It is in my library but not on my Kobo

Open the book's page: it says where the book stands on your Kobo. Not on it or you deleted it on the Kobo: press Send to my Kobo (or Put it back on my Kobo) and sync. It arrives at the next sync but it never does: Send it again. Nothing on the page at all: Devices → Kobo → Test my link.

A book disappeared from my Kobo

It is still in your library. Either you finished it and chose Finished books leave the Kobo (Your settings), you removed it on the Kobo, or your Kobo gets only the books you send. Its page → Send to my Kobo brings it back, and it stays. My books → Not on my Kobo lists every such book.

My Kindle never gets the mail

The library's sending address must be on your Amazon approved list (see Kindle). Devices → Send a test shows whether it works.

The wrong book (or edition, or language) arrived

Its page → Wrong book. It leaves your library and the portal finds another copy, asking you first.

Search finds nothing

Try the author's surname and one word of the title, or Pick in Shelfmark, which searches every source. Very new books often appear a few days after release.

Why am I asked to confirm every copy?

Release names are only labels someone typed. Showing you the copy first means a wrong edition never lands on your Kobo. The admin can let certain picks go through without asking.

Can someone see what I read?

No. Each library is private. When you get a book the library already holds, it is simply added to yours; nobody is told, and nothing says whose it was. A reader who keeps their books private (Your settings) is never counted.

Something else is wrong

Tell the admin what you did and what you saw; a screenshot of Requests helps.
