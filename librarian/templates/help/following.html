Follow a book series, an author, or a comic or manga series, and anything new that comes out appears under New for you on your portal home page, with one tap to get it. Nothing downloads by itself, and following a long series never floods you: only what comes out from now on is shown.

  • Book series and authors: Following → search → Follow. Open a name to see every book in order, which ones you have, which are ready now (no download), and get the missing ones.
  • Comics and manga: the Follow button on a series page in Comics.
  • Each followed name is checked once a day. Books are only announced once they are out, not when merely announced.
  • Your Hardcover "Want to Read" list can feed the portal too: turn it on under Devices, and books you add there become requests waiting for your yes.
