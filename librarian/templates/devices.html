Before you start (read once)
  • While linked to this library, the Kobo's own store and OverDrive/Libby stop working on the device.
  • Signing out, signing in again or a factory reset rewrites the config file: put the link back afterwards.
  • Books sync as EPUB (or KEPUB); on a colour Kobo, comics sync as fixed-layout Kobo copies ("Make Kobo copy" on a comic's page if one is missing). PDFs are downloaded from My books.
  • The first sync of a big library takes minutes; leave the Kobo on Wi-Fi until it finishes.

Preferences

{{ csrf_field() }}
Copies in another language are never fetched for you automatically.

Formats: the library converts books to EPUB on arrival (PDF and comics stay as they are). Kobo receives EPUB over sync — it reads normally, but the Kobo only remembers your place at each chapter, not the exact page. Choose KEPUB above and books you download for your Kobo come converted, with exact page tracking. Kindle accepts EPUB or PDF by e-mail, AZW3/MOBI only over USB.
