  • While linked to this library, the Kobo's own store and OverDrive/Libby stop working on the device.
  • Signing out, signing in again or a factory reset rewrites the config file: put the link back afterwards.
  • Only EPUB (and KEPUB) books sync to a Kobo; PDFs and comics can be downloaded from My books.
  • The first sync of a big library takes minutes; leave the Kobo on Wi-Fi until it finishes.
